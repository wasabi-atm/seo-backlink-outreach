## Read this first
- **No page was opened.** The sandbox blocks every website (motiontheagency.com and client sites return 403), so all research is from web-search results. URLs are real (each appeared in a search result), but only titles/snippets were seen. Confirm each page mentions video before outreach.

- Motion's blog list came from search results, not a full crawl, so better matching posts may exist.
- Where the client is a past customer, the warmest route is a link to Motion's own case study about them, or a free animated graphic for their page.
